# Example: input + actual output

Validated test run of the fixed prompt in `SKILL.md` (2026-10-11). All regression cases in `evals/evals.json` pass against this output.

## Input

```
Words float around in my head like golden drifts. They are delicate and jumbled, and when I try to speak, they get stuck. My little sister babbles all day, but nobody understands her either. The cat scratches the sofa whenever it is angry. Mom made lemonade, and the smell of the tangy lemons filled the kitchen.
```

## Output

### float around
- POS: phrase
- Why it is hard: figurative — words cannot really float, so the picture of moving inside the head is not literal.
- Meaning: The words seem to move slowly here and there inside the writer's head.

### golden drifts
- POS: noun
- Why it is hard: rare word and figurative; "drift" is usually a verb, but here it is a noun in a simile.
- Meaning: Piles of light things that the wind pushes together. Here the writer says her words are like soft, shining piles in her mind.

### delicate
- POS: adjective
- Why it is hard: rare word for this learner's level.
- Meaning: Very light and fine, easy to break or lose.

### jumbled
- POS: adjective
- Why it is hard: rare word, and it comes from the verb "jumble" but works as an adjective here.
- Meaning: Mixed together with no order.

### get stuck
- POS: phrase
- Why it is hard: the whole phrase means something different from the words alone; it does not mean put glue on something.
- Meaning: The words stop and will not come out when the writer tries to speak.

### babbles
- POS: verb
- Why it is hard: rare word for this learner's level.
- Meaning: Makes many sounds like talking, but the sounds are not clear words.

### either
- POS: adverb
- Why it is hard: common word with a different grammar role here — at the end of a negative sentence it means "also", while learners mostly know "either ... or".
- Meaning: Also. It follows a "no" or "not": nobody understands the sister too.

### scratches
- POS: verb
- Why it is hard: word outside this learner's list, and it takes the -s verb ending here.
- Meaning: Makes thin marks or small cuts on the sofa with its claws.

### whenever
- POS: other (conjunction)
- Why it is hard: looks like the question word "when", but here it means "every time" and joins two parts of the sentence.
- Meaning: At any time that; every time.

### lemonade
- POS: noun
- Why it is hard: word outside this learner's list; the ending "-ade" does not help guessing.
- Meaning: A cold sweet drink made from lemons, sugar, and water.

### tangy
- POS: adjective
- Why it is hard: rare word for this learner's level.
- Meaning: With a sharp, fresh, lemon-like taste.

### filled
- POS: verb
- Why it is hard: common word used with an unusual meaning — there is no container here; the smell goes everywhere through the kitchen.
- Meaning: Went into every part of the kitchen; the smell was everywhere in it.
